Before takeoff

Five things that ruin a mapping flight

The sky can look clear and the flight still go wrong. This is what the app checks for you, hour by hour.

Low sun

Long shadows hide detail and shift from one pass to the next.

Partial clouds

The light changes between photos and the mosaic comes out patchy.

Wind and gusts

The drone drifts off its flight line and burns more battery.

Rain

It soaks the gear and ruins the photos.

High Kp

Geomagnetic activity makes GPS and RTK unstable.

FAQ

What people usually ask

What does the app check for a mapping flight?

Wind and gusts, the sun’s height (ideal light from 40°; below that it warns you about shadows), partial clouds, rain and the GPS Kp index. You can adjust the limits to your drone.

Does it work for photos and aerial video?

Yes. The same factors make a good camera flight: even light, manageable wind and steady GPS.

Why can a fully overcast sky come out as good?

Because it gives even light, with no hard shadows. What the app flags with caution are partial clouds, which make the light change from one photo to the next.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, pick “Mapping drone” and your field, and you see your window.

Is it free?

Yes. The hour-by-hour verdict, 3 days of forecast, the map with your fields and the morning notice are free and never expire. Pro adds 14 days, window alerts, all your fields and the decision log.

Does the app decide for me?

No. The forecast is indicative and the decision to fly is always yours.
