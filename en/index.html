Five things that ruin an application

Your weather app shows you the sky. To spray, you need all of this checked against your method, hour by hour.

Gusts

They push droplets off the field and carry them where they shouldn’t go.

Temperature inversion

A mist of fine droplets hangs in the air and travels far.

High Delta T

In heat and dry air, droplets evaporate before they land.

Rain in the next hours

It washes the product off before it works.

High Kp

Geomagnetic activity makes your drone’s GPS unstable.

Mapping drone

Flying to map, photograph or film?

With the Mapping drone method the question changes: it’s no longer when to spray, but when to fly with good light and steady GPS.

  • Sun height: ideal light from 40°, no long shadows
  • Partial clouds that make the light uneven
  • Wind and gusts, with more margin than spraying
  • Kp index for GPS and RTK

FAQ

What people usually ask

Is SprayClarity free?

Yes. The hour-by-hour verdict, 3 days of forecast, the map with your fields, the morning notice and the WhatsApp report are free and never expire. Pro adds a 14-day forecast, window alerts, all your fields and the decision log.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, pick your method and your field, and you see your window.

Which application methods does it support?

Agricultural drone, ground sprayer, backpack sprayer, motorized mist blower and mapping drone. Each comes with its own limits, and you can adjust them to your equipment and product.

Does it work for ground sprayers?

Yes. With “Ground sprayer” the verdict uses its own limits: wind from 2 to 9 mph and gusts up to 16 mph, plus Delta T, temperature inversion and rain. You can adjust them to your boom and product.

Does it work for mapping drones, photos and video?

Yes. With “Mapping drone” the app checks what matters when flying with a camera: wind and gusts, the sun’s height, partial clouds that make the light uneven, rain and the GPS Kp index.

Where does the data come from?

From public weather models (ECMWF, GFS and ICON) via Open-Meteo, plus NOAA’s geomagnetic Kp index, which affects drone GPS.

Does it work without signal in the field?

Yes. With no connection, the app shows the last saved forecast and lets you know you’re offline.

Does the app decide for me?

No. The forecast is indicative and the decision to spray is always yours. SprayClarity shows you each factor so you can decide with more information.

Which countries does it work in?

Anywhere with a forecast. People already use it in 42 countries, in Spanish, English, Portuguese and French, with metric or imperial units.

How do I go Pro?

In the app, on the plan card. You pay with your Google Play or App Store account and can cancel anytime from the store.
